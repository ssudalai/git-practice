Kazi Nazrul Islam

The national poet of Bangladesh






Rebel poet

Kazi Nazrul Islam (1899–1976) was a Bengali poet,
musician, and revolutionary who is considered
one of the most prominent literary
figures in Bengali literature and the national poet
of Bangladesh. He was also a significant cultura
l icon in India, particularly in the Bengali-speaking regions.

Awesome writer

Nazrul's works explored themes of revolution,
freedom, and the struggle against oppression.
His poetry often reflected his strong
sense of social justice and his support for the
rights of the oppressed and marginalized communities
